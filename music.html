NAVE AMIGA

Band shooting with three members of an experimental metal band.

Raw textures, colours, red lights and black & white — a little universe between noise and silence.

Nave Amiga is an experimental metal band. In October I shot three of its members, looking for images as heavy and strange as their sound.

Between colour, red light and black & white, each picture plays with contrast, tension and atmosphere.
